About me

I am a postdoctoral researcher in the Department of Electronic Engineering at Tsinghua University, working with Prof. Yong Li at the Future Intelligence Lab. I received my Ph.D. (2026) and M.Eng. (2022) from Tsinghua University, advised by Prof. Yong Li, and my B.Eng. from Tianjin University (2019).

My research lies at the intersection of artificial intelligence, complex systems, and computational social science. I study how people and intelligent algorithms shape one another, how collective behavior emerges in networks of LLM agents, and how we can build useful artificial societies for social science.

My work has appeared in Nature Machine Intelligence and Nature Communications. I lead the development of AgentSociety, an open research platform for large-scale social simulation and computational experiments.

Research interests

Human–AI Interaction

Feedback loops between people and algorithms, information cocoons, and the social impact of recommendation.

LLM Agents & Social Intelligence

Social cognition, opinion dynamics, and collective behavior in networks of intelligent agents.

Computational Social Science

Large-scale artificial societies and reproducible experiments for social and economic systems.
